# Royal Material and Suppliers

A modern web-based Material Management and Invoice System for construction material trading businesses.

## 🏢 Company Overview

**Royal Material and Suppliers** is a construction material trading company that deals in the purchase and sale of various building materials, including:

- Cement
- Bricks
- Sand
- Stone Dust
- Crushed Stones
- Aggregate
- Rait
- Gravel
- Steel Bars
- Other Construction Materials

The system is designed to simplify daily business operations such as inventory management, customer management, purchase tracking, sales management, and invoice generation.

# 🎯 Business Objective

The primary goal of this application is to digitize the day-to-day operations of a construction material supplier by providing:

- Centralized inventory management
- Easy purchase and sales tracking
- Professional invoice generation
- Customer and supplier management
- Financial ledger maintenance
- Real-time stock monitoring
- Business analytics and reporting

This system helps reduce manual work, minimize errors, improve operational efficiency, and provide better financial visibility for the business.
